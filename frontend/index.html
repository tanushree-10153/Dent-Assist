Our Story

We Make Dental Care Accessible

Dent Assist was built by students of Terna Engineering College with one goal — make it easier for people to find and book trusted dental professionals without the hassle of long waits or confusing processes.

We connect patients directly with verified dentists across multiple specializations. Whether it's a routine checkup or a specific treatment, our platform makes the entire journey smooth — from search to confirmation.
